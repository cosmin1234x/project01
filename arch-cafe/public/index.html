Our little corner

A bit of Central Europe,
in the heart of Cornwall.

At Arch Cafe we serve brunch all day, so a late start is never a problem. Some of it is proper comfort food from home: golden Hungarian lángos, Slovak baked rice pudding. The rest is café classics done properly, like poached eggs on sourdough, a good BLT and a strong flat white.

Everything's made fresh, portions are generous and there's always something vegetarian or gluten-free on the board.
